Privacy Policy

Blokaholic · Last updated: 4 October 2026

The short version

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No ads.
  • No tracking, analytics, cookies or similar tracking technologies.
  • We do not collect, receive or sell any personal data.
  • Your scores and settings stay on your own device.

Who we are

Blokaholic ("the game", "we", "us") is a block puzzle game that you can play in a web browser at blokaholic.app or as an app. The game is operated by Blokaholic, based in the United Kingdom, which is responsible for this policy. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at blokaholic.help@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, login, sign-up or contact forms. It does not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tracking tools, and it does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. Gameplay never sends information about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or other device permissions.

What is stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data only on your device, in your browser's local storage (or the app's equivalent storage). This includes:

  • your best scores, daily streak and daily results;
  • simple game statistics, such as games played and lines cleared;
  • unlocked themes and skins, and progress points;
  • settings such as sound, music and your chosen theme;
  • whether you have already seen the tutorial hints.

Local storage is used only to save your game on your device. This is strictly necessary to provide the game you have asked to play, so no consent banner is needed. This data is never sent to us and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Sharing scores and challenge links

If you choose to share a score or challenge a friend, the game creates a message and a link on your device. The link contains only a game seed (a number that decides the pieces), the game mode and your score, for example ?c=1qztwvf-c-k3. It does not contain your name or any other information about you. Score images are also generated on your device.

Nothing is shared unless you tap a share button. The message goes through whichever app you pick (for example a messaging or social app), and that app's own privacy policy applies to it.

Hosting

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service run by GitHub, Inc. Like most web hosts, GitHub may automatically log technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests the game's files, for security and to operate the service. We do not have access to these logs. GitHub delivers the site through its content delivery network partner Fastly. Both are based in the United States, so this technical information may be processed outside the UK under GitHub's own safeguards. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The game contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no chat with other players.

Your rights (UK GDPR)

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you have rights over your personal data, including the right to access, correct or delete it. Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to provide or delete. The data stored on your device is under your control, as described above.

If you have a concern, please contact us at blokaholic.help@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, by adding analytics or online features), we will update this policy before the change takes effect and change the "Last updated" date above.
